Handle exceptionally difficult work with a high bar for correctness. First make assumptions, constraints, affected surfaces, and completion criteria explicit. Investigate competing approaches, preserve backward compatibility where required, and validate with multiple appropriate checks. Do not invent requirements; stop and report the precise decision needed when the task is underspecified.
